# TCP reels · batch 1

Fourteen reels for TikTok and Instagram, and the welcome video for new members. Each reel comes with
sound and without, and has a cover:

- `-voice.mp4`: the voiceover, subtitles and an original music bed. Post as it is.
- `-sound.mp4`: no sound. Add a trending sound in TikTok or Instagram, over the on-screen text.
- `-music.mp4` (reels without a voice): an original TCP track, if you'd rather not use a trending sound.
- `-cover.jpg`: the cover frame. Choose it as the cover in the app.

All are 1080 × 1920 at 30 fps, and loud enough for TikTok and Instagram (−14 LUFS). Text stays clear
of each app's buttons and caption. Everything is made from scratch: the voice is AI (Kokoro, a free
open model), the music is synthesised, and the app footage is the real terminal in demo mode, labelled
on screen.

## Before you post

- **Label the AI voice.** On TikTok, turn on *AI-generated content* (More options) for `-voice` files.
  On Instagram, add the *AI info* label. It's the platforms' rule, and it fits TCP: honest numbers.
- **Trending sounds and business accounts.** A TikTok *Business* account can only use the Commercial
  Music Library: still plenty, but not every trending sound. On Instagram, a *Creator* account keeps the
  full music library; a *Business* account gets fewer songs. The `-music` files are ours, so they're
  always safe to use.
- **Keep the reels educational.** None of them tells viewers to open a broker account, and that's
  deliberate. In the UK, promoting CFD brokers on social media is a regulated financial promotion, and
  the FCA has prosecuted finfluencers for it. Let the link in bio lead to the bot, which shows the
  partner disclosure and risk warning before anyone joins. Get advice before posting "open an account
  and join" content aimed at UK viewers.
- **End every caption with the risk line**, as below.
