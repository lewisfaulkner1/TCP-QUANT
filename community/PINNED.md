## 3. The leaderboard

```
🏆 THE TCP LEADERBOARD

See how you trade, and join the leaderboard, by connecting your MT5 read-only.

HOW
1. Open the terminal → Account → Connect MT5
2. Enter your account number, server and your INVESTOR password (not sure what it is? Tap "Where's my investor password?" on that screen)
3. Pick a name for the leaderboard (or keep your stats private)

WHAT'S SHARED
Percentages only: each day's gain or loss and how many trades won and lost. Never your balance.

IS IT SAFE?
• The investor password is read-only: it can see trades, never place them or move money
• It's encrypted on your phone. The terminal itself can't read it
• Never enter your master (trading) password. If you do by mistake, it's refused and you'll be told to change it
• Disconnect any time: your link and every day recorded are deleted

The tables show each trader's worst day and open trades beside the gain. No prizes for the biggest gain: size your risk as if nobody is watching.

For live PU Prime and Vantage accounts.
```

## 4. FAQ

```
❓ FAQ

Is this financial advice?
No. Everything here is education and information. Every trade you take is your decision, and your risk.

Do I have to take every signal?
No. Take the ones that fit your plan and your risk. Many traders risk 1% or less per trade.

How do I open the Quant Terminal?
Tap Terminal in your chat with the bot, or the link in the Quant Terminal post above.

Why does the terminal show odds?
So you can see how likely a level is to be reached before you trade it. They're probabilities, not promises.

Are the results real?
Every Playbook setup is logged before the result and followed to the end. Nothing is deleted, and the record is chained, so any later edit would show.

Can I connect a prop firm account to the leaderboard?
Not yet: it's for live PU Prime and Vantage accounts.

Someone DM'd me offering to manage my money. Is that TCP?
No. The team will never DM you first asking for money, passwords or logins. Report and block them.

Still stuck? Ask in 💬 Lounge or message the team.
```
