# TCP on TikTok and Instagram

How to set up the two accounts, so they look like one brand and every lead is tracked. The reels,
their captions and the first two weeks' posting plan are in `brand/video/reels/POSTS.md`.

## Accounts

**TikTok: a Personal account.** It keeps every trending sound. A Business account gets a link in bio
sooner and more analytics, but only the Commercial Music Library. TikTok expects businesses to use
that library, so for anything that promotes the Inner Circle directly, use a Commercial Music Library
sound or our own `-music` track.

**Instagram: a Creator account** (Settings → Account type and tools). It keeps the full music library;
a Business account gets fewer songs.

Link the two in Instagram (Accounts Centre) and in TikTok (Edit profile → Instagram), so each profile
shows the other.

## Name and handle

- **Name:** `TCP · The Crypto Playbook` (25 characters; both apps allow 30).
- **Handle:** the same on both, and short enough to say out loud. In order of preference:
  `@tcp.playbook`, `@thecryptoplaybook`, `@tcp.quant`. Check both apps before you choose: a matching
  handle matters more than the exact wording.
- **Profile picture:** `brand/logo/official/profile-picture-crown-1080.png`. The crown alone reads at
  the tiny size a profile picture is shown at. Use the same one on both.

Partner links · High risk · Not advice · 18+
```

Instagram (150 at most; this is 136):

Trading maths, gold & the Quant Terminal
Partner link ↓ I may earn commission. High risk. Not advice. 18+
```

Keep the partner and risk words in both. The link leads to the bot, which is where people are asked
to open an account, so the bio says what it is.

## The link in bio

One link each, to the onboarding bot, tagged by platform so every lead card shows where it came from:

| Where | Link |
|---|---|
| TikTok bio | `https://t.me/<your bot>?start=tt` |
| Instagram bio | `https://t.me/<your bot>?start=ig` |
| Instagram story link sticker | `https://t.me/<your bot>?start=ig` (sticker text: **Inner Circle**) |

The bot does the rest: the 18+ check, the partner disclosure and risk warning, the broker, and
verification. Send `/stats` in the team group to see the scoreboard, with `tt` and `ig` side by side.

TikTok may not show a clickable link until the account has 1,000 followers. Until then, write
`Telegram ↓ link on Instagram` in the bio.

## Rules for every post

1. Education, not selling: the reels teach. Joining happens through the bio link and the bot.
2. Every caption ends with the risk line. Any caption that asks people to open an account also carries
   the partner disclosure.
3. Label AI: the voiceover reels use an AI voice. Turn on TikTok's *AI-generated content* setting and
   Instagram's *AI info* label for them.
4. Demo is labelled. App footage with demo prices or data says so on screen and in the caption.
5. No results that aren't real. Nothing about members' profits unless it's measured, in full, with the
   losses.
6. Never promise returns, and never say "guaranteed", "risk-free" or "easy money".
7. Reply to comments, and to questions with a video when you can.
8. UK viewers: a post that invites people to open a broker account is a financial promotion, which
   the FCA regulates. Keep those posts off the socials until you've had advice.

## Replies to have ready

- **"What's the link?"** "In our bio: it takes you to our Telegram bot, which walks you through."
- **"Is this a signal group?"** "It's a trading community with a quant terminal and a Playbook where
  every setup is logged before the result. Wins and losses."
- **"How much do you make?"** "We don't post profit screenshots. The Playbook's record is measured
  against pure chance, losses included."
- **"Can you manage my money?"** "No. We never manage money, and we'll never DM you first. Anyone who
  does isn't us."
