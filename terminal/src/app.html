A zone counts as reached when price trades into it after the brief goes out. The turn is how far price then went back the way it came, less how far it went on through, before the session closed, in average daily ranges (ATR). Moving at random, price reaches each zone as often as its odds say and goes back as far as it goes through, so zero is the line to beat.

How it's measured

  • Closed trades only, read from each account's own history with its investor (read-only) password.
  • Each day's gain is on the balance at the start of the day. Deposits and withdrawals don't count as gains or losses.
  • Weeks and months compound the days. The day table is the last full trading day, which ends at 17:00 New York.
  • Each trader's worst day and open trades show beside the gain: a list of gains alone rewards gambling, and closed trades alone can hide losers left open.
  • Consistency is the average day over how much the days vary, scaled by how many there were. Above 2 over 20 or more days, luck alone gets there about 1 time in 40.

No prizes for the biggest gain: size your risk as if nobody is watching. Past results don't predict future returns. Trading carries risk.
